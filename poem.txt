In a world full of bugs, bad design, and despair,  
There was Math, spreading sunshine and fixing the air.  
She sees ten more ways when we've settled for two,  
Making “good enough” go, “Wow, that's brand new!”

But lately, things haven't felt right,  
The work's lost its sparkle, the days aren't as bright.  
Perhaps they don't see all the magic you bring,  
Or appreciate your urge to improve everything.

But listen, my friend, here's a thing to remember,
A cloudy forecast doesn't cancel December.
Don't shrink your big ideas to fit someone else's frame;  
Or trade in your curiosity just to play their game.

So keep asking questions, keep caring so much,  
Keep that beautiful, stubborn “Let's fix this!” touch.  
And when the world feels grey, remember what's true:  
The place may not see your light, but we do.

You're Math — the creator, the spark, and the flame,
Still wonderfully YOU, whatever the name,
Of the company, team, or the role that you play.,
Keep being your sunshine. The world needs your rays. ☀️,
